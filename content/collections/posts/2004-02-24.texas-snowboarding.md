<div id="entrytext"><a class="imagelink" href="http://zaskoda.com/wp-content/uploads/2006/09/8.jpg" title="Virtual Snow In Action - Taken from snowflex.com"><img align="right" alt="Virtual Snow In Action - Taken from snowflex.com" id="image18" src="http://zaskoda.com/wp-content/uploads/2006/09/8.thumbnail.jpg"/></a> Being from Texas, snowboarding has always meant long trips to somewhere that actually has both mountains and snow. For a long time, I pondered and discussed the idea of an artificial snowboarding surface. In my mind, some kind of little styrofoam balls seemed like the right way to go. Eventually, I caught a reference to artificial turf in a snowboarding magazine - thus confirming that it does indeed exist.Then, while checking out <a href="http://www.snowgo.com/" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="Thanks for the link Snowgo!">Snowgo</a>, I found my way to <a href="http://www.theboarder.co.uk/news/article.html?newsid1070888556,64836," rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="The Boarder Article on Artificial Snow Surface">this article</a> and eventually to the website for <a href="http://www.snowflex.com/" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="SnowFlex - Maker of Virtual Snow">Snowflex.</a> This stuff looks pretty cool. Unlike my idea of lots of tiny balls, these surfaces are more like astroturf with extra padding. According to the article, it doesn't ride like snow, but it's "pretty darn close!" It sounds like fun regardless.But that article was about a resort in the UK. I've dug around for more info on artificial snow turfs and it seems it isn't used at all in the US. This is ironic because <a href="http://www.summitdaily.com/apps/pbcs.dll/article?AID=/20040223/NEWS/402230105&amp;rs=1" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="Summit Daily - Snowboarding's humble beginnings">snowboarding was invented in the US</a>. If I could go to somewhere close and ride a day on artificial turf for a reasonable price, I'm certain I'd be there at least a couple times a month. Does anyone have any investment capitol they need to get rid of?

I tell ya, I'd like to do it... I'd like to bring Snowboarding to Texas!

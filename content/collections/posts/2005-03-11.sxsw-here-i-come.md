Tomorrow morning I'm off to the <a href="http://2005.sxsw.com/interactive/">SXSW Interactive Festival</a>. Many of the interactive panels have sparked my interest including: <a href="http://2005.sxsw.com/interactive/conference/panels/?action=show&amp;id=IAP0071">The New New Economy</a>, <a href="http://2005.sxsw.com/interactive/conference/panels/?action=show&amp;id=IAP0017">Web Design 2010</a>, <a href="http://2005.sxsw.com/interactive/conference/panels/?action=show&amp;id=IAP0010">Digital Convergence In Central Texas,</a> and <a href="http://2005.sxsw.com/interactive/conference/panels/?action=show&amp;id=IAP0078">Open Source Marketing</a>. The interactive segment of the event has really grown since I spoke there in 2001. Even Wired is <a href="http://www.wired.com/news/digiwood/0,1412,66828,00.html?tw=wn_tophead_2">talking about it</a>.

Time to finish packing and snuggle into bed, the 5 hour drive awaits me in the morning.

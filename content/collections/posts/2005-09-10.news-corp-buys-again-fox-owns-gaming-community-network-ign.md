News Corp must have a plan. Either that, or they're still operating off of that mentality that drove so many investors to waste cash during the bubble. Recently <a href="http://zaskoda.blogspot.com/2005/07/fox-buys-myspace-big-money-in.html">I mentioned</a> that News Corp picked up Myspace for $580 million. This time, <a href="http://www.abcmoney.co.uk/news/102005875.htm">News Corp purchased the massive gaming community network IGN</a>. The price tag on <a href="http://www.ign.com/">IGN</a>? How about $650 million! Myspace and IGN are completely different networks. Myspace was a home-grown social network. IGN is a collection of news sites, online tools, and various communities. For a while, IGN was in the buying game as they picked up various sites and communities to add to it's collection. Apparently, the strategy worked.

This is - of course - nothing new. Both <a href="http://www.google.com/">Google</a> and <a href="http://www.yahoo.com/">Yahoo</a> have been snatching up communities lately. Ironically enough, I'm posting on blogging software that <a href="http://bayosphere.com/blog/dangillmor/">Google bought</a>. While everyone may have expected Google, Yahoo, and even Microsoft to be players in the online community market - it seems that News Corp is moving in on their cyber territory. (yes, I said cyber).

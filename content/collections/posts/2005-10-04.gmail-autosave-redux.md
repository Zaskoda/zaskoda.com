Back around March of this year, <a href="http://hirevito.com/">Vito Miliano</a> created <a href="http://hirevito.com/greasemonkey/humanegmailautosave.html">a greasemonkey "Autosave" script for Gmail</a>. It was a great idea and solved a very annoying problem.

This morning I logged into Gmail and noticed the "<a class="nfc" href="http://mail.google.com/mail/help/about_whatsnew.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">New! Auto-save and more</a>" link in bright red at the top of the page. Turns out, Gmail went ahead - some 8 months later - and added an autosave.

I had to post and give some public kudos to Vito for being ahead of the curve on this one. ;)

So, I'm wanting to find out more about this projected called <a href="http://www.citadel.org/">Citadel</a>. It's a groupware package for lots of important stuff like calendars, email, and chat. I actually found it while looking for a good calendar server solution. Citadel is more than a calendar for sure, the site says it can be used like a <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bbs">BBS</a>.

While learning more, I ended up reading <a href="http://www.citadel.org/index.php?option=com_content&amp;task=view&amp;amp;amp;id=96&amp;Itemid=41">this blog post</a>. It is a must read, particularly for anyone who remembers the BBS scene (or is still involved). The idea that MySpace is just another BBS is completely accurate. I'll quote another good point:
<blockquote>Even the "BBS's are from yesteryear" groupthink over at Slashdot is particularly ironic, considering that Slashdot itself is basically just a big BBS optimized for the reporting and discussion of tech news.</blockquote>
So for all you peeps who ran a BBS in your basement purely for the joy - you can now build one on the Internet and sell it for over <a href="http://zaskoda.blogspot.com/2005/07/fox-buys-myspace-big-money-in.html">half a billion dollars</a>. I'm sure, forum centric products like vBul are not the answer. A forum is but one small aspect for a virtual community. Custom baked community software rules until something more modular and diverse than our typical forum software comes along.

Perhaps Citadel is a good foundation for something like that. I will be checking into it.

This reminds me of a rather annoying moment on my last new years snowboarding trip. Some dunce busted out his phone in the front of the busy lift line and started chatting it up. His conversation had him engaged to the point where he had no interest in getting on the lift, yet he stood there blocking up the line. The lift operators had to grab him and physically move him out of the way. As far as I'm concerned, they should have kicked him off the mountain. Maybe if he had this hat, he would have been able to jump on the lift during his oh so important call - likely with his mommy.

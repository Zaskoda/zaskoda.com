If you read my journal, you know that I recently bought a new snow tripping vehicle that I keep intending to post pictures of. While on my search for this vehicle, I found a Texas Nissan dealership that likes to sell big trucks. Let me show you them.

<a class="imagelink" href="http://zaskoda.com/2006/12/21/big-texas-trucks/gnarly-looking-armada/" id="p121" rel="attachment" title="Gnarly Looking Armada"><img alt="Gnarly Looking Armada" id="image121" src="http://zaskoda.com/wp-content/uploads/2006/12/gnarly.thumbnail.jpg" style="float:right"/></a>The first one to catch my eye was a modified Armada that actually looked pretty sweet. I guesstimated the gas mileage to be around 12mpg. They really struck the right height on the lift and I actually like the chrome (rare for me). If I was the type of self absorbed wealthy fool it took to purchase an H2, I think I would rather waste my money on this thing.

<a class="imagelink" href="http://zaskoda.com/2006/12/21/big-texas-trucks/tan-armada/" id="p124" rel="attachment" title="Tan Armada"><img alt="Tan Armada" id="image124" src="http://zaskoda.com/wp-content/uploads/2006/12/tan_giant.thumbnail.jpg" style="float:right"/></a>There were actually many modified Armadas on this lot. The Armada is based on the Titan pick up truck frame. They are Nissan's huge SUV. They normally run upwards of $50k before you start adding mods. I thought this tan one looked kinda crappy. The rear seems to sit kinda low, I don't like it.

<a class="imagelink" href="http://zaskoda.com/2006/12/21/big-texas-trucks/internet-equiped-armada/" id="p122" rel="attachment" title="Internet Equiped Armada"><img alt="Internet Equiped Armada" id="image122" src="http://zaskoda.com/wp-content/uploads/2006/12/internet_cattle.thumbnail.jpg" style="float:right"/></a>One of the Armadas, you could just live in. Along with all of the standard 'make it look tough and tall' mods, they also put a satellite dish on top. Now you can watch pay per view or browse the web while you're burning enough gas to power a small country.

<a class="imagelink" href="http://zaskoda.com/2006/12/21/big-texas-trucks/semi-or-pick-up/" id="p123" rel="attachment" title="Semi or Pick Up?"><img alt="Semi or Pick Up?" id="image123" src="http://zaskoda.com/wp-content/uploads/2006/12/semi_or_pickup.thumbnail.jpg" style="float:right"/></a>Finally, one of my favorite mammoth Texas trucks on this lot just happened to be a Ford. It was the first F550 I've ever seen. I don't know if this thing comes stock at this unreasonable height, or if it was lifted. The tires on this rig look like they rolled right off of a semi. There are two steps for climbing up into this creature. It's nuts. I forgot to look at the price.

So yeah, things really are bigger in Texas.

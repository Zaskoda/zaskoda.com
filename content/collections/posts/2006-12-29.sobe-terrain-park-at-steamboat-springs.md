<p>We really dig the Sobe terrain park here... It's easy to get a view across the park from the top and there's a variety of stuff to play on. The best part is the lift dedicated to taking you up to the top of the park (or pipe).</p>
<p>Today Velzy and I also found a smaller park with the tiniest little rail I've ever seen... thing was no more than 2 feet long. It also had a nifty mini-pipe that we both tried out. I think the walls were somewhere in the range of 4 or 5 feet tall. It was just enough to get a feel for going up the walls and turning around.</p>

<p><a class="imagelink" href="/blog/waves-from-santa-cruz" id="p179" rel="attachment" title="Zaskoda Surfing Cowells"><img alt="Zaskoda Surfing Cowells" id="image179" src="http://zaskoda.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/04/spost_cowells.thumbnail.jpg" style="float: right"/></a>Snowboarding is my first love. Somewhere behind that, I've found an appreciation in surfing; and that's how I spent my Easter holiday. Me and Velzy flew out to San Fransisco, rented a car, and drove down to <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Cruz%2C_California">Santa Cruz</a>. This is a special place to her. While it's not the only place she's lived; when someone asks her where she's from, she says Santa Cruz. I know now why she loves it so much. It's not just the breath taking views and fantastic weather. The people I met in Santa Cruz were wonderful. The vibe and energy are unlike any other place I've been. We surfed for three days - <a href="http://www.surfline.com/reports/report.cfm?id=4189">Cowells</a> once and <a href="http://www.wavewatch.com/Live-Surf-Cams-Region.php?CamID=18&amp;RegionID=4">Pleasure Point</a> twice. We saw the <a href="http://www.mysteryspot.com/">mystery spot</a>. We ate sushi at Pink Godzilla. I bought a pair of sandals at <a href="http://www.oneill.com/">O'Neill's Surf Shop</a>. We checked out the <a href="http://www.santacruzsurfingmuseum.org/">Santa Cruz Surf Museum</a>. I saw an otter play and seals sunning on rocks. Our hotel made us fresh cookies and brownies. It was good times. I'll be back.</p>

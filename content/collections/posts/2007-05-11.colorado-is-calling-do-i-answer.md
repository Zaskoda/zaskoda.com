<p><a href="http://zaskoda.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/05/me-at-boulder.jpg" title="Me at Boulder Falls"><img alt="Me at Boulder Falls" src="http://zaskoda.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/05/me-at-boulder.thumbnail.jpg" style="float:right"/></a>It's been a crazy couple of weeks. I quit my job after 3 years of hard work. Tonight I'm unemployed and having a spot of trouble sleeping. I'm facing a variety of decisions and I'm running out of "think about it" time. One of those decisions has the potential to land me in Colorado. For the first time in my life, I might be able to snowboard all season long.</p>
<p>The company who'd have me flew me up for an interview. I decided to hang out for a couple of extra days and explore. It was one of those experiences where everything just kept going right. I felt peaceful. To my surprise, I've yet to feel excited. Locally, other opportunities have come up, including a window that would allow me to start my own business. I've got options.</p>
<p>I don't know where all this is taking me.</p>

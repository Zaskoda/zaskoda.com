<p><a href="/blog/my-first-full-season-0708" rel="attachment wp-att-214" title="Lake View in Colorado"><img alt="Lake View in Colorado" src="http://zaskoda.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/06/colorado_view.thumbnail.jpg" style="float: right"/></a>It looks like my first full season of snowboarding will be 07/08. I accepted the job offer near Denver and will be moving this Sunday. I may get wordy about the job opportunity and adventures in the future, but for now I'll just say it's related to <a href="http://zaskoda.blogspot.com/">community development</a>.</p>
<p>I should be less than 2 hours from Winter Park with other slopes even closer. I expect my stay in the area to last around a year and a half - at most three years. I'm excited to see how much my riding will improve during this phase of my life. I expect that, at some point, I will finally get my fill of snowboarding and be ready to pour my energy into something new. I'm sure I will always snowboard, but I think that a few seasons will cure my obsession.</p>

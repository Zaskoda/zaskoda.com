In Sept of last year, I posted <a href="http://zaskoda.blogspot.com/2006/09/myspace-beginning-of-end.html">Myspace: The Beginning Of The End</a>. The post was a mix of my personal observations along with a little bit of supporting news media. Last Friday, MSNBC picked up on the trend in a story titled, "<a href="http://www.msnbc.msn.com/id/19735915/">OMG! YR still on MySpace? Loser!</a>" Awesome title, and a good read. There is a growing trend of users seeking out communities with a sense of integrity and purpose. MySpace has neither.

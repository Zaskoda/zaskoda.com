<p><a href="http://zaskoda.com/2007/07/17/another-interview-with-charlie-aaron-of-bearfire-resort/bearfire-lodge-2/" rel="attachment wp-att-231" title="Bearfire Lodge"><img alt="Bearfire Lodge" src="http://zaskoda.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/07/bearfire-resort-lodge.thumbnail.jpg" style="float:right"/></a>I just ran across a June 12th <a href="http://theeightfold.com/?p=300">Interview with Charlie Aaron</a> over on <a href="http://theeightfold.com/">t h e e i g h t f o l d</a>. Hvgill writes:</p>
<blockquote>My fascination isn’t because it’s some interesting new business model or new way of approaching theme parks. It’s not even the peculiarity of building an outdoor year-round winter attraction in a state known for summers over a hundred degrees.</blockquote>
<p>I was recently grilled by a forum goer regarding the potential environmental impact of the resort. This interview sheds some new light on just how green this project really is! </p>
<blockquote>"I think it’s good to note from the get-go that a facility the size and scope of Bearfire will have no portion of its’ operation that will emit any type of gases or contaminants."</blockquote>
<p>As a side note, I was particularly tickled to see the author of the interview use the term "<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lohas">LOHAS</a>". As I've mentioned before, I recently relocated to the Denver area to get in a season or two of snowboarding. I just happened to be working for <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaiam">Gaiam</a>, the company that originally coined the term. Small world.</p>
<p>Anyway, go read the <a href="http://theeightfold.com/?p=300">interview</a>!</p>

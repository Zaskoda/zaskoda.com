<p><a href="http://zaskoda.com/2007/08/29/96-days-left/chair-lift-at-volcano-villarricapucon-chile/" rel="attachment wp-att-253" title="Chair Lift at Volcano Villarrica/Pucon, Chile"><img alt="Chair Lift at Volcano Villarrica/Pucon, Chile" src="http://zaskoda.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/08/villarica_lift.thumbnail.jpg" style="float: right"/></a>So I was at the office talking about my <a href="http://zaskoda.com/2007/07/01/100-days-of-boarding/">goal of 100 days</a>. I mentioned the <a href="http://http://zaskoda.com/2007/08/20/video-from-pucon-chile/">Pucon trip</a> and said I wasn't sure if I should count it towards my goal. My co-worker replies, "of course you should!" And thus it was so! I am now counting it towards the season. Hey, lets face it, 100 days is a lofty goal - I'm going to need all the help I can get.</p>

<p>We just got the first touch of snow here in Colorado recently. We had a storm blow through about a week ago that dropped a light dusting in some of the higher parts of the mountains. We've still got around 3 months before resorts really start to open up with good snow. However, I've been getting some information on places to ride early. I hear <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berthoud_Pass">Berthoud Pass</a> gets some of the first snow. It's the pass full of switchbacks between I70 and Winter Park. Hopefully, I can get a few early days in there.</p>

<p><a href="http://zaskoda.com/2007/08/29/snow-magazine-interview/bearfire-village-artist-render/" rel="attachment wp-att-255" title="Bearfire Village Artist Render"><img alt="Bearfire Village Artist Render" src="http://zaskoda.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/08/village_01.thumbnail.jpg" style="float: right"/></a>I received and interesting phone call yesterday. Someone from <a href="http://snowmagazine.com/">Snow Magazine</a> found some of my posts regarding the <a href="http://www.bearfireresorts.com/">Bearfire Resort</a> project and hunted me down. I have noticed in the past few weeks, a lot of traffic hitting my site is coming via google searches for information on the resort. All this buzz makes it feel that much more "real". If I were still down in Texas, I'd go out, find the site where they're building, and snag some photos. Anyway, I'm excited to see if I get quoted in print. Come to think of it, I'm not entirely sure she ever asked for my real name. Furthermore, she didn't really ask me anything that I hadn't already discussed <a href="http://zaskoda.com/category/texas-snowpark/">here on the site</a>. Still, I was stoked to get the call.</p>

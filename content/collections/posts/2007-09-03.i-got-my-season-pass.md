<p><a href="/blog/i-got-my-season-pass" rel="attachment wp-att-261" title="My Colorado Pass"><img alt="My Colorado Pass" src="http://zaskoda.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/09/pass.thumbnail.jpg" style="float: right"/></a>I decided to swing by Ski and Golf as this weekend marked the start of the big sales. While there, I found a booth selling the <a href="http://www.snow.com/passsales/info/coloradopass.asp">Colorado Ski Pass</a>. I'd been looking at them online - so they didn't really need to sell me on them. What I didn't know was that you can't buy them online - you can only renew them. They have to take your photo for the pass. I'm lucky I happen to stop by today as the prices will be going up soon.</p>
<p>An interesting note about the pass - after donating the $1 for some kinda parks and wildlife thing, the pass cost exactly $420.00 after taxes.</p>
<p>Bring on the season!</p>

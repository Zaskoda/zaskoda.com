It's still October, the MIDDLE of October, and <a href="http://www.arapahoebasin.com/?page=site/news_template&amp;nav_id=a31253b49c9bbfeb8eaac83ef2bddf1e">Arapaho Basin just opened!</a> Lucky me, I have no plans for the weekend. It's one run and all man made, but I'll go slide down it a little bit. Saturday and Sunday will give me 2 more days towards my count, bringing me down to 94. I planned two trips over the following two weekends. One trip is to see my family and friends back in Texas. The other is to go surfing with my dear friend Velzy in Santa Cruz. I probably should have married her by now but... anyway... Including this weekend, I have 4 free weekends between now and Thanksgiving - when I originally thought I would begin the season. That's potentially 8 days of boarding bringing me to 86 as the "real" season begins. With this kind of luck, I might actually make my goal!</p>

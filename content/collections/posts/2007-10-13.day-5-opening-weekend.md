<p><a href="http://zaskoda.com/2007/10/13/day-5-opening-weekend/opening-weekend-at-a-basin/" rel="attachment wp-att-273" title="Opening Weekend at A-Basin"><img alt="Opening Weekend at A-Basin" src="http://zaskoda.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/10/opening_day.thumbnail.jpg" style="float: right"/></a>Joe, Christina, and I went up to a-basin for opening weekend. As expected, it was crap! But, of course, we had fun. There was one run with all man made snow. It was rather crowded. My board got slammed into repeatedly, but I was riding my old beater board (<a href="http://http://zaskoda.com/2006/11/11/my-kemper-storm-156/">the Kemper</a>) so I didn't care. We only took a few runs before we got tired of falling on hard pack and getting run into. Still, it marks my 5th day of snowboarding this season, leaving me with 95 to go to reach my max goal. My minimum is 36 days, so I've got 31 left there. The minimum should be a snap. I still can't see how I'll make the 100, but I'm gonna keep after it.</p>

<p>I was doing some hard drive cleanup before running off to Thanksgiving dinner and I ran across an old video. Several of my recent posts have had subtle and not-so-subtle references to someone I like to call "<a href="/blog/my-surf-history">My Surf Teacher</a>" in this blog. The video was a montage I made of her learning to snowboard over the years.</p>
<p>Sharing life and experiences with this person for the last five years has been a blessing. Generally speaking, I have a lot to be thankful for. I have a good life, good friends, a good job, and a good family. However, when I sit down in front of a dead bird with new friends tonight, I will thank the universe, most of all, for all the time I've gotten to spend with the star of this video:</p>
<object height="355" width="425"><param name="movie" value="http://www.youtube.com/v/CiUwsJmrc3I&amp;rel=1"/><param name="wmode" value="transparent"/><embed height="355" src="http://www.youtube.com/v/CiUwsJmrc3I&amp;rel=1" type="application/x-shockwave-flash" width="425" wmode="transparent"/></object>
<p>Can you tell she's a long boarder? She makes those long smooth carves. Maybe we'll ride again some day. I hope so. For right now, I'm just asking the universe to give her peace of mind and a lots of joy. In fact, I'm sure she's catching one perfect wave after another today. That makes me smile. Time for me to go eat Turkey!</p>

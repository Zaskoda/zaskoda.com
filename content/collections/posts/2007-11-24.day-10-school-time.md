<p><a href="http://zaskoda.com/2007/11/24/day-10-school-time/being-silly-in-the-lift-line-2/" rel="attachment wp-att-293" title="Being silly in the lift line."><img alt="Being silly in the lift line." src="http://zaskoda.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/11/being_silly1.thumbnail.JPG" style="float: right"/></a>I just got back from my 10th day of the season! I only have to do this 9 more times and my goal is accomplished! Today was a longer day on the mountain; but it was an easy going day. I got to play teacher. The morning started out good, as usual. Unfortunately, the crowds hit again at noon. We still don't have enough snow to open a lot of runs so everyone is packed into a small space. I don't think we're expecting another good snow any time this week. I'm glad I got in the days I could while I could. Of course, I'll head out again tomorrow. Things are, however, looking unpleasant for Keystone 36 next weekend. I'm sure I'll still go, but it will be a lot nicer if we get some more snow.</p>

<p><a href="/blog/getting-ready-for-the-weekend" rel="attachment wp-att-296" title="Xterra Packed For The Night"><img alt="Xterra Packed For The Night" src="http://zaskoda.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/11/packed_up.thumbnail.JPG" style="float: right"/></a>I'm currently at a <a href="http://pragmaticstudio.com/">Ruby on Rails conference</a> in Denver. As soon as they let us out, I'm on my way out to <a href="http://keystone.snow.com/info/keystone36.asp">Keystone 36</a>. I have no idea what to expect. I'm not even sure where I'm going. I am stoked about the idea of <a href="http://keystone.snow.com/info/nightski.asp">night riding</a> as I've never tried it before.</p>
<p>I have friends coming in for the weekend, but no specific plans to hook up with anyone. Instead, I've decided to do something a little crazy. I cleared out the back of my Xterra and laid down the seats. Then I packed up all my gear along with two sleeping bags, pillows, blankets, food, and other supplies. My intention is to spend the night in the back of my truck.</p>
<p>This conference is awesome, but I'm eager to get on the road as I hear they're expecting a lot of snow in the mountains tonight!</p>

<p><a href="http://zaskoda.com/2007/12/19/taos-opens-to-snowboarders/taos-opens-to-snowboarders/" rel="attachment wp-att-310" title="Taos Opens To Snowboarders"><img alt="Taos Opens To Snowboarders" src="http://zaskoda.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/12/ride_taos.thumbnail.jpg" style="float: right"/></a>On March 19th, one of the last remaining 'Ski' only mountains, <a href="http://www.skitaos.org/">Taos</a>, will <a href="http://www.ridetaos.org/">open it's doors to snowboarders</a>. Coming from someone who skied and snowboarded both of the neighbor resorts (<a href="http://www.angelfireresort.com/winter/">Angelfire</a> &amp; <a href="http://www.redriverskiarea.com/">Red River</a>), there's really only one thing to say... <strong>Holy Crap Dude</strong>...</p>
<p>Honestly, I never thought this would happen and had come to terms with it a long time ago. Check out the <a href="http://www.ridetaos.org/">Ride Taos link</a> and, while you're there, be sure to swim through the comments!!!</p>

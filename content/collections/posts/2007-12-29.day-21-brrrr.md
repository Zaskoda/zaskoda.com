<p><a href="/blog/day-21-brrrr" rel="attachment wp-att-320" title="1 degree F at A-Basin Today"><img alt="1 degree F at A-Basin Today" src="http://zaskoda.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/12/abasin_is_cold.thumbnail.png" style="float: right"/></a>I hit A-Basin today. It was cold. It's been cold all week, but today it was windy too. Right now, according to <a href="http://www.weather.com/outlook/recreation/ski/snowreport/303001">weather.com</a>, it's 1 degree F and it 'feels like' -20. It was cold enough that I was comfortable wearing my helmet today. I even shut the vents on it. I hit some black mogul runs that I'd previously been avoiding. All in all, it was a good day. I would have stayed longer, but freezing wind just isn't my thing. It's supposed to warm up tomorrow - hooray!</p>
<p>As a random side note, I sure am looking forward to next year!</p>

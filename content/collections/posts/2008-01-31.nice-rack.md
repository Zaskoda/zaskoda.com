I dropped in and, sure enough, they had a good piece of hardware to use as a snowboard rack. I've been trying to find something for a while. I've even considered building something from scratch. I'm rather handy, I just don't have a lot of tools available. Anyway, I think this will do nicely. It's not as space saving as I had hoped for, but the shiny chrome sure looks snazzy. (not really, I hate chrome, but whatever, I'm content)</p>

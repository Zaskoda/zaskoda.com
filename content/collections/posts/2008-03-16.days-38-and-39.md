<p><a href="http://zaskoda.com/2008/03/16/days-38-and-39/echo-mountain-road-sign/" rel="attachment wp-att-366" title="Echo Mountain Road Sign"><img alt="Echo Mountain Road Sign" src="http://zaskoda.com/wp-content/uploads/2008/03/echo_mountain_sign.thumbnail.jpg" style="float: right"/></a>I went out to <a href="http://breckenridge.snow.com/winterhome.asp">Breckenridge</a> on Saturday (yesterday). I rode with Becky, Jason, and Jayne. We had some good snow fall the night before - around 16 inches I was told. Most of the snow was already tracked down; but, it was still soft enough that falling didn't hurt. The weather was nice and it was good to get a full day in. I'm still building back up from being sick. The highlight of the day was having a squirrel dart across the run in front of me.</p>
<p>Today I went up to <a href="http://www.echomtnpark.com/">Echo Mountain</a>. I rode solo. I was really nervous most of the day. By the time I was relaxed and confident, I was getting tired. I was happy to see all the brand new Echo Mountain road signs. The little park finally got big enough to get their signage. I really like Echo, it has a fantastic vibe.</p>

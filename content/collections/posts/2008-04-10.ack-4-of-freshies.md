After riding hard pack yesterday, this is killing me. I should have worked yesterday so I could ride today! Either way, my snowboarding clothing is all in the wash so I'm not going anywhere for the next hour or three. I have a 5:00 appointment this evening but I'm thinking of trying to sneak off to Echo for a few slides. Oh, the unfairness of it all!</p>

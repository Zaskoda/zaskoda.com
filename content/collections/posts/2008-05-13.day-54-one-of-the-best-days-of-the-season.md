<p><a href="http://zaskoda.com/2008/05/13/day-54-one-of-the-best-days-of-the-season/abasin-on-may-13th-2008/" rel="attachment wp-att-403" title="Abasin on May 13th 2008"><img alt="Abasin on May 13th 2008" src="http://zaskoda.com/wp-content/uploads/2008/05/abasin.thumbnail.jpg" style="float: right"/></a>I was supposed to be in Mexico today, but my flight got delayed until Wednesday. Thank goodness! The <a href="http://www.arapahoebasin.com/">A-Basin</a> website is reporting only 5 inches of fresh powder, but it sure seemed like a lot more to me. The snow conditions today were fabulous. They're expecting even more snow tonight. I simply can't believe that it's May. Everyone was talking about how the conditions seemed more like mid-season. I pushed powder until my thighs were burning.</p>
<p>I wish I could enjoy the next few days, but I'm off to Sayulita tomorrow to do some surfing. I'll be back in Colorado in a week.</p>

I haven't been up since Sunday. However, last Sunday, at Keystone, was my 100th day snowboarding - of all time. After checking the weather reports the night before, I decided to go ahead an wear more than just my snow pants. It seemed cold, so another layer was warranted. Well, I under estimated. After scraping 8 inches of fresh snow off my truck, I ran inside and put on every layer I could find. We've had <a href="http://www.aspentimes.com/article/20081219/NEWS/812199978/1077&amp;ParentProfile=1058&amp;title=More%20snow,%20cold%20head%20for%20Aspen">a lot of fresh snow fall around here; but it's very very cold</a>.

All in all, it was a good day. However, I am always surprised at how quickly Keystone gets packed and icy. I love a lot of things about Keystone - the nice new gondola, the A51 terrain park, a lot of extra terrain in the back; but the snow generally isn't on the list. I often times give up faster lifts and extra terrain to shred A-Basin less than 5 minutes away.

So, now that I've hit my 100th day ever, I will probably stop counting. It was a fun metric for a while, but I'd like to redefine my goals in terms other than days on the slopes. Here are some things currently floating around in my skull:
<ul>
<li>Get avalanche survival certification.</li>
<li>Ride some back country. Maybe start with Loveland Pass.</li>
<li>Ride somewhere I need to wear snowshoes to reach.</li>
<li>Do a snowcat trip.</li>
<li>Do a heli trip.</li>
<li>Land a backside 180 and a 360. Currently, I've only landed front side 180s.</li>
<li>Truly ride the pipe, maybe even learn to throw a trick or two.</li>
<li>Ride Heavenly, Taos, Brighton, Kicking Horse, Whistler/Blackcomb, Park City, and Mt Bachelor.</li>
</ul>
Maybe I'll think of some more goals later... but I think that's a good list to start with.

A while back, <a href="http://zaskoda.com/2009/04/01/the-next-big-thing-is-an-appliance/">I posted about an appliance that will change our lives</a>. I described the vision as a piece of hardware that would sit at your home entertainment center and do lots of neat stuff. In the comments, I showed a couple of desktop machines that fit the bill from the hardware perspective. (Many of these machines are being called nettops now.) Today, I saw a bit of news that fits for the 'other half' of the problem.

<a href="http://arstechnica.com/software/news/2009/04/boxee-gains-an-app-store-to-appear-on-hardware-in-2010.ars">Ars is reporting</a> that <a href="http://www.boxee.tv/">Boxee</a> is gaining an apps store (has a new API for Plugins!) and is getting it's own hardware next year.

I'm definitely not going to blog about this every single day. Nor am I going to track calories for every single meal. If I had a handy calorie tracker, I might. Anyway, today's food was much in line with yesterdays. The surgar cravings definitely kicked it, but I've been allowing myself an orange after each workout. This is definitely a higher calorie approach than <a href="http://iride.shobek.com/2007/11/12/hardcore-fitness-training/">the last time I did this</a>. Still, with today's bike ride, I nearly passed out. I was definitely at the edge of my envelope. I also got a chance to weigh in today, I'm definitely heavier than I have been in a long time.

<strong>Diet</strong>

Breakfast: 1/2 cup Grapenuts , 1/4 cup Sweetened Almond Milk, 2 shots espresso

Lunch: 1 plate at Indian buffet consisting of : 1/4 cup cooked rice, hefty salad, about 1 spicy grilled chicken breast,  2+ cups steamed veggies, 1 serving spoon of that yummy creamy spinach curry (noms!)

Afternoon Snack: 1 cup carrots &amp; celery, 2 shots espresso

Dinner: large chunk of grilled salmon with onion and lightly crusted with ground oats, string beans, black beans, grilled zucchini, small slice of sharp cheddar, 1 emergency pack, 1 multivitamin, 1 Flexamin

Post Workout Snack: 1 orange

<strong>Exercise</strong>

3.2 miles mountain biking (accumulative 709 vertical feet of climbing)

<strong>Measurements</strong>

Weight: 178 lbs

I love snowboarding. I <a href="/blog/categories/snowboarding">write about it</a> a lot. It didn't take me long to run into <a href="http://www.shaunwhite.com/projectx/?fbid=2wcw6LdJOOd">Project X</a>. You can't really pay any attention to snowboarding and not know who Shaun White is. So the concept is kinda neat - they built Shaun his own half pipe in the back country. Mostly, it just makes me kind of hate the guy - only for the shallow reason of jealousy. So many of my friends scrape together to try to afford passes and trips while this guy is being flow in via helicopter to his own private pipe. That might inspire 8 year olds, but it pretty much just makes me bitter.

So, yesterday on Twitter, I got a message from <a href="http://twitter.com/alyssadilor">@Alyssa</a>. It's a sparse account. The account was created in Feb, but there are no tweets in all of April and only one in May. Her message to me was:
<blockquote>@<a href="http://twitter.com/zaskoda" style="text-decoration: none; color: #2276bb; padding: 0px; margin: 0px;">zaskoda</a> hey, sweet blog! love the personal info. i'm working on a ridiculous snowboarding that you might be into. DM me to find out more!</blockquote>
This was followed by:
<blockquote>@<a href="http://twitter.com/zaskoda" style="text-decoration: none; color: #2276bb; padding: 0px; margin: 0px;">zaskoda</a> a ridiculous snowboarding project** excuse the typo!</blockquote>
<!--more-->Which, interestingly enough, is the most recent post for that account. I followed and DM'd. She DM'd back a link to Project X. I followed up by asking more about her involvement in the project and website with no reply.

This morning on facebook, I had the following message from <a href="http://www.facebook.com/carrie.milford">Carrie Milford</a>:
<blockquote>Hey Scott,

Awesome website. That idea to turn your van into a camper is a cool idea.

My name is Josh and I'm working with Red Bull on a project called Red Bull Project X.

It's a private halfpipe that was built for Shaun White hidden in the backcountry of Colorado. This allowed Shaun to practice and learn some never-before-seen tricks without media interference. There is a YouTube video explaining it a little more: <a href="http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e1Zoh2JC_XA" onmousedown='UntrustedLink.bootstrap($(this), "b07a5666680ad3c9fc4018ec297419b3", event)' rel="nofollow noopener" style="cursor: pointer; color: #3b5998; text-decoration: none;" target="_blank">http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e1Zoh2JC_XA</a>

We built a website to show the project to the public, and it's live now at www.redbullprojectX.com. Click on "New Tricks" to see the latest. These tricks are insane!

We hope your readers would be interested in this content. If you think it's something they would like to see, it'd be awesome if you could post a link and let your readers know about it. If you don't feel this is a good fit, don't worry about it and thanks for your time.

Whenever we have new updates to the site (new tricks, pics &amp; stuff) I'll be sure to keep you posted. Check out the site and let me know what you think!

Sincerely,
Josh</blockquote>
That's right, Josh! The next message I got was from <a href="http://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=14229087">Josh Wigod</a>:
<blockquote>Hey Scott,

For some reason the facebook account was logged into someone elses account. If you want to hear more about Project X just shoot me an e-mail at josh@brandingbrand.com

-Josh</blockquote>
Josh has over 1,000 friends. Impressive, eh? If you're interested, check out <a href="http://brandingbrand.com/">brandingbrand.com</a> - the domain used in his email address. I noticed he wasn't listed on their <a href="http://brandingbrand.com/contact-branding-brand.html">contact page</a>. My guess is that Carrie forgot to log out of her personal Facebook account before working her fake Facebook account. Woops!

What bothers me is not being contacted about this project, as it is relevant to my interest. The thing that bothers me is the complete lack of integrity and authenticity. All they had to do was contacted me with an authentic voice and say, "hey, we're promoting this project and would love a mention!" I would have respected and appreciated that.

I follow a lot of folks on twitter, facebook, and blogs that are heavy into snowboarding. Most of them have a much stronger readership than I do. Real authentic networking could definitely work in Branding Brand's favor here. But this, it makes Redbull and Shaun White look pretty lame.

By the way, I hate Red Bull. Salt, sugar, and caffeine - yuck. Too bad it's so hard to find <a href="http://energy-drink-ratings.blogspot.com/2006/08/sobe-adrenaline-rush-energy-drink.html">Sobe Adrenaline Rush</a> these days. It's the only energy drink worth drinking... but that's another story.

So I'm curious, who else is getting these random messages from Branding Brand?

I met her almost two years ago now. It was about 6 months before we went on our first date. It didn't take long for the two of us to become a couple. Today is the first day since then that I'd openly say we weren't a couple.

I moved out a month ago. Our intention at the time was for it to be a break up. We didn't really break up and I liked it that way. Last night we had that long conversation and I gave her back her house keys.

Today I'm a complete mess on the inside. It's hard to see my own life clearly right now. For the past month I've gone home, to my new home, and retired to my room with this creepy feeling that I need to pack up and go back to my real home, where we lived together. Sometimes it's hard to leave work and not instinctively drive directly to her place.

My last relationship, before this one, was 5 years of on-again off-again madness. There was a lot of hurt.

The world sped up, it spun so fast I almost flew off. My heart beat so heard, I thought my chest to explode.

Chemicals rushed, hormone raged in ways I've rarely known. Humility, as I learned I am not the stable center of the universe. Fear, as I found myself a single particle flying through space.

Reaching, for every bit of knowledge I've ever known. Reacting to every emotion. 

I embraced the negative feedback loops until my body shook from terror and fear. I went to places I thought I'd never see again. I went to place I never want to be again.

So many eyes, so many words, so many caring souls telling me the things my knowing already knew, but I couldn't hear over the noise of my thinking.

For so many months I tried to break the attachment. I thought I was too weak to go for what I wanted. The tearing, the ripping, the screaming, and the bleeding as the bonds came lose whether I was ready or not. Only to find the freedom I thought I was reaching for was an idea, and old idea that I was attached to. As the illusion fail, the attachment died.

And here I am, adrift in space, floating. It's so beautiful here, but part of me still feels anxious. From here I can see everything so clearly, and I don't like all of what I see. I want to cry.

I look back and I see... I see the love I had. I see the joy. I see a woman that chose me. I see a life I was building that my heart has always wanted. A warm home, beautiful pets, good friends, stability, support, love... and most of all, her. I see that it's all gone now.

I look at now and I see hope... I see that my issues are not that great. I see where investing this energy in myself can help me grow beyond anywhere I've been before. I see where, this regrettably painful experience was the catalyst for me to get back to the work I've done for so many years. 

I remember asking the universe for a break, a reprieve.  Years have passed and it was a reprieve. For a time, it was the best life I could imagine myself having. My artistic nature was fed and encouraged. I saw my slight visions birthed into reality. 

But most of all, I looked into the eyes of a woman and for the first time felt that connection, that love, that knowing I had only dreamed about. I mean that in the most literal possible way, for I have long had dreams where a woman - always different in appearance - would come to me. The feeling, the knowing, was always the same. And I, in my waking life, got to experience that feeling... looking down into the eyes of my lover, realizing fully who they were... being completely in the moment, and loving her for everything that she was. Total contentment, total peace, and total knowing.

That moment is now a memory. My knowing fades in and out of reception as my thinking ramps up and then settles back down. I feel the peace, the inner peace and balance.

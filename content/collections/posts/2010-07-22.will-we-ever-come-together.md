I'm pondering an idea tonight about community, family, and society. I've recently realized the importance of growing my social network and really getting to know my friends. I want to integrate myself as a useful and supportive part of my social environment. This is 100% contrary to my path for the past two years where I've been breaking bonds and getting rid of "stuff"...

My recently relationship left me wanting. For the most part, I'm through the painful stuff. I did so without engaging some sexual offers. These offers all came from women who had established partners. I also was honest with those who truly liked me and were available. I'm not truly emotionally available and, as tempting as it is, chose not to mislead. No single woman presented herself as single, available, and interested in having some form of mutual rebound type relationship.

Too personal? Probably... At first I was very jaded at the seeming inability of women to "be single". I got over that. However, I was still left with a loneliness. I want to be held. I'm still in a state of withdrawal. From what I remember, the first month is hard, the second month is crazy hard, and then it all gets easier. It's been just over a month now. 

Through this process, I've talked with many friends, some with good advice but... mostly, a whole lot of "yeah, I've been there, it sucks dude..."

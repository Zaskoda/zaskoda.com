I've wanted to write about Megan for some time now, but I haven't been able to put down words that felt meaningful. 

I fell in love with Megan at first sight. When I spoke to her, we connected immediately. I courted her briefly. It was beautiful. Then she left my life for a time. When I fell in love with Megan, I had no doubt I would some day marry her. Months later, when she was gone from my life, I couldn't understand how I could be so wrong. It was one of two times in my life when I felt total certainty about my feelings towards a woman.

When she came back into my life, she was attached to another. It would be the man she would eventually marry. Meanwhile, for a decade we talked about life at a level I rarely get to with others. We knew each other. 

Last summer, I knew something was wrong when she kept telling me how happy she was. We finallly stopped talking to each other. When we did this parting of ways, we were on slightly bitter terms. It was very uncharacteristic of us. 

As a teen, I lost my brother after a fight.. This effected me. I was deeply scared it would happen again. This made it hard for me to allow someone space to process an emotionally intense interaction between us. I wanted to reach a peaceful resolution before giving them space for fear they would leave and never come back.

We had no mutual friends. About 9 months after she shut me out, a stranger on Facebook sent me a message telling me she was gone. It was nearly a day later before he told me it was suicide, but I already knew. Many of my close friends already know, because of the death of my brother, the death of a loved one while on bad terms is one of my deepest fears. 

Megan was physically beautiful. She was also a beautiful soul. She was hyper intelligent and often frustrated. As that stranger on Facebook said, she was pure light. I was always in love with her, even when I was fully in love with other people in my life.

Megan couldn't be single. As another put, she had difficulty "holding love". I have a little of this myself, and was one of many levels on which Megan and I knew each other.

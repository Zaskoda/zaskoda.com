<a href="/assets/blog/2011/02/pb_rounded.jpg"><img alt="" class="alignright size-thumbnail wp-image-5537" height="150" src="/assets/blog/2011/02/pb_rounded-150x150.jpg" title="Polar Bear In Driveway" width="150"/></a>It's been over 9 months since I <a href="/blog/rebuilding-the-bear">last blogged about Polar Bear's status</a>. A lot can happen in 9 months. I've invested a fair amount of <a href="http://farm5.static.flickr.com/4120/4874103229_24a2c41597_b.jpg">blood</a>, sweat, and tears.

I laid down <a href="http://farm6.static.flickr.com/5209/5262992032_c7abe71875_b.jpg">new flooring</a>. Currently I have <a href="http://farm5.static.flickr.com/4080/4734465339_7b85bca418_b.jpg">bed liner coating</a> against the metal, a <a href="http://farm5.static.flickr.com/4123/4881702008_699ce1e6cd_b.jpg">foam</a> layer next, <a href="http://farm6.static.flickr.com/5162/5219701732_3fd8bba38d_b.jpg">wood</a> on top of the foam, and finally a <a href="http://farm6.static.flickr.com/5008/5262382369_eccfb8f24b_b.jpg">vinyl mat</a> on top. Ultimately, I'll lay carpet in key areas. This is the single biggest improvement in sound dampening yet.

For a while, I installed a <a href="http://farm5.static.flickr.com/4093/4827884654_f3e668bf11_b.jpg">roof top tent</a> on the <a href="http://farm5.static.flickr.com/4120/4827276077_f6c085d283_b.jpg">roof rack</a>. The rack was a huge project, but cost me less than $150.

<!--more--><a href="/assets/blog/2011/02/fruita.jpg"><img alt="" class="alignright size-thumbnail wp-image-5538" height="150" src="/assets/blog/2011/02/fruita-150x150.jpg" title="Polar Bear in Fruita at Night" width="150"/></a>All 5 doors now have <a href="http://farm5.static.flickr.com/4051/4651548650_0f88f7aab8_b.jpg">wood panels</a> (soon to be covered in vinyl). The rear door and slider are filled with insulation. The <a href="http://farm5.static.flickr.com/4078/4865250868_120c414cde_b.jpg">walls and ceiling are covered in Reflectix</a> and will be further insulation and paneled. I made a <a href="http://farm6.static.flickr.com/5164/5262383007_21e542b986_b.jpg">doghouse cover</a> with thick fabric also improving sound quality greatly. Door seals have all been replaced, though this has created its own set of door issues that I'm still working through.

Both captains chairs are now installed with <a href="http://farm5.static.flickr.com/4081/4902840490_53ce286c62_b.jpg">kick ass seat covers</a>. The passenger seat swivels to the back. I left the driver's seat fixed on a boxed base so I can use it for storage. I also attached a <a href="http://farm6.static.flickr.com/5094/5416162315_b7028e97ed_z.jpg">mag light pointed towards the fuse box</a>.

I've added <a href="http://farm6.static.flickr.com/5180/5391791578_e574d07efd_z.jpg">mirrors</a>, <a href="http://farm6.static.flickr.com/5284/5302260142_28193754fa_z.jpg">fog lights</a>, and other odds and ends. I repaired a bit of electrical work including the <a href="http://farm6.static.flickr.com/5300/5390554803_5da6d49ab1_z.jpg">rear brake lights</a>. I've slowly been replacing bulbs with LEDs.

The best thing is just how much I've gotten to USE Polar Bear. I've used her like a van for tons of projects like <a href="http://farm5.static.flickr.com/4090/5216612975_54937a45f0_b.jpg">building a haunted house</a>. I took her to <a href="http://farm5.static.flickr.com/4049/4702620154_17545c76cc_b.jpg">Apogaea</a>, to <a href="http://farm5.static.flickr.com/4153/4991919352_ab63b091f5_b.jpg">Burning Man</a>, through <a href="http://farm5.static.flickr.com/4090/4991938614_f728c69664_b.jpg">Moab</a>, mountain biking in <a href="http://farm6.static.flickr.com/5204/5212750201_f78bb22efb_b.jpg">Fruita</a>, up <a href="http://farm6.static.flickr.com/5162/5365002621_3b09e168a9_b.jpg">snowy passes</a> just outside of Boulder, and on more than a few snowboarding trips.

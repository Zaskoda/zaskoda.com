I took apart an old printer and a few other pieces of electronics to collect the gears for the photo above. My team and I ran around the office that day. We offered everyone a bite of "<a href="http://www.gearboxity.com/component/option,com_easygallery/act,categories/cid,44/Itemid,44/">geareal</a>" and took their picture for the website. It was almost as much fun as <a href="http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GcaOJjX1mMw">Spybox</a>. That project was popular enough to spawn a <a href="http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IiT7s9rAv-g">sequal</a>!

That was years ago. Two days ago, I decided to log back into my forum account. I dug up some old threads (not overly popular of forum communities) and poked around. I found the thread that was my 1005 post! Talk about nostalgia.

I tried logging in again a few minute ago. They suddenly deleted my account altogether. o.O

I put a lot of energy into that company and <a href="http://zaskoda.com/2005/06/08/a-few-good-forum-ideas/">community</a>. I met some of the most amazing people in my life while working there. I feel leaving that company was one of the best career decisions I've made. For what it's worth, everyone in my department ended up moving on to better companies. Today was a brilliant reminder as to why.

Once piece at a time, Polar Bear is turning into something interesting. I've had endless set backs and challenges. This weekend, I was finally able to install the roof top tent. This will, I hope, be my home at Burning Man this year. I must say, time is short and there's much left to do. I'm having a little bit of trouble planning and organizing my attack. It's particularly difficult when the van spends so much time in the shop. If she's not stable in time for Burning Man, I will need a back up plan on the quick.

I want to build a larger tent structure that mounts to the van's roof rack. It would need to be something I can easily assemble by myself. It should provide shade around the van and perhaps some additional coverage for the tent.

I need to seal the inside of the van better. I've covered the metal in bed liner. Next I need to insulate and build the floor and wall panels. I think I can re-use the old ceiling panels. I sure hope I can cover it all in vinyl before the burn.

I would like to get the secondary battery and some exterior lighting setup before the Burn, but this feels ambitious. I don't want to risk significantly changing the electrical system right before two and a half weeks in the desert.

Rock sliders, exterior bed liner coating, bumpers, and oh so many other modifications will have to wait until sometime after Burning Man. Meanwhile, I have all of the standard Burning Man preparations to deal with. From costumes to water containers.

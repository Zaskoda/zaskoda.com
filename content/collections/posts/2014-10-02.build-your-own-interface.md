This idea we have that everyone should design their own website interface is just plain backwards. When the Web first became popular and geeks flocked in droves to build their first sites, they were welcomed by an easy to learn system called HTML. There were no web design experts or companies. Everyone became a "designer"...

Fast forward, decades later, to early 2012. There's an industrial force of professionals dedicated to crafting website interfaces. We have widely used applications such as Facebook where the interface changes uniformly across the entire userbase at seemingly random intervals. Meanwhile, there exist no significant feedback method for the users of the interface to influence this change.

When we all became designers, we got rather egoistical about it. This sense of competition worked to our favor, we rather quickly built the Internet of today. But look what we've done. Every resource has a different interface. Sometimes the interfaces break, blocking you from some bit of information you needed. Some resources work well on your mobile device, but many don't. It's a real mess.

You like making things, don't you? Would you like to have the controls over your own interface? View every online resource in a consistent interface. Your interface. You configure the views and you tweak the controls. The resources are no longer websites, but rather web APIs. Look at the exact same data on your mobile device, your TV, your laptop, your tablet, and even the LCD in your car. Customize each device however you prefer.

Just an idea...

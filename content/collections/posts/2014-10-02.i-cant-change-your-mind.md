This song, Congratulations, by Blue October probably means a lot of things to a lot of different people. I remember how I first heard it. My house mate in Plano, TX danced around the house blasting the whole Foiled album. She loudly sang as she cleaned, "you lift me." As it turns out, the lyrics are, "you left me." This song so painfully accurately reflects  I have allowed self pity, doubt, and fear to hold me back for years, perhaps decades.

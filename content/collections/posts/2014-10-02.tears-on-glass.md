I reached to draw a smiling face until the eyes dripped away. As the tears were streaming, I drew the smile upside down. Slowly the day's sun melted the face away leaving the clear view of those breath taking snow covered Flatirons.

Moment to moment, I felt a familiar cycle in and around me. I can't say that I did anything different. I surrendered to watching the patterns, as I sometimes do. Wouldn't you know it, a subtle shift... a hiccup... something to smile about. I've always lived a blessed life, even when I've felt cursed.

Wasted like that moment I reached the pier at the end of a hard fought swim and felt my fingers lose grasp of the wood as I slipped under and gasped in a lung full of water. Where's the hand this time? Where's that strong hand that reached into the water before and lifted me to the shore?

She talked about it for years. She thought about it for years. How could it be a surprise, she posted about it on the Internet. She saw no value in the experience and she exercised her right to leave.

She's wrong... she's so wrong, every fiber of my being insisted. There's something here, something worth it. Then my life crumbled to nothing.

I fought and I cried and I grew and I tried. I found myself abandoned by the very foundation of my support, free falling into self destructive pity and a complete loss of hope. She was right... every fiber of my being insisted... sans just enough to keep me bound here.

I fought for a year. I turned every ounce of intention towards this notion of healing. I took the pilgrimage and I found nothing but selfishness, gluttoy, and hedonism. I took the slow, long drive home.

Again, I pooled what I had and, instead of the trek of a pilgrimage, I decided to manifest the vessel close to home. Through my glorious success and a mission accomplished, I took a sniper shot to the anatomically correct core of my circulatory system. Scabs on scabs. Bruises on bruises.

So much beauty, joy, and love... so much caring, support, and oh so many beautiful people... So much that is so worth while...

Yet again, the most significant confidant in my life reached in to that old familiar throb... one she's touched so softly and so gently so many times... I felt so close to healed... so warm and safe... so happy with myself... so happy with what I had been a part of.

And now... I feel so blind to the miracle that happened... because I feel the need to stop and lick this wound. Ignoring it just didn't seem to work. Like that small scratch that suddenly feels like a life threating gash the moment you actually notice the blood dripping, all I can seem to focus on is this pattern.

Over and over and over... the only common factor is me... is it me? Or was she right? Is life a tragedy by nature?

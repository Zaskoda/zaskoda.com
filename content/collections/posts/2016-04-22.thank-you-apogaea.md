Apogaea is Colorado's regional Burning Man event, it is the organization that throws it, it is the members of that organization, and it is the vast community of people who identify as Apogaeans. From the outside, Apogaea is a festival, a gathering, a camping trip with lots of art. From the inside, Apogaea is a microcosm.

I discovered Apogaea in 2008.

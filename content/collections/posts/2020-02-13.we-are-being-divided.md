<p>I'm on vacation at a beautiful lake with my favorite person in the world and we just had an argument about politics. Somewhat ironically, we have rather well aligned political views. </p>
<p>Just yesterday I had a remarkably unusual Facebook argument with an old friend. Despite our rather minor level of disagreement, we managed to talk entirely past each other. In general I feel I have been having more and more unsettling arguments with friends and family. As someone who tends to cross social and political boundaries often, I've become somewhat accustom to heated engagements. It feels as through the flavor of these engagements soured significantly and relatively recently. </p>
<p>Where I am at right now, there are no televisions or radios on. The loudest sound is the wind in the trees. My partner is laying in the sun on the dock. But my emotions are still drawn back into a tension I can't stop feeling. Something seems more wrong than usual.</p>
<p>I tend to be somewhat intellectually confrontational. I'm not especially well informed nor am I significantly above average intelligence. So my confrontational approach often leads me into painful realizations of my own level of ignorance and my ability to race to assumption. I love that particular kind of pain as I believe it's my personal best chance at becoming a more-informed person. But those engagements, as I mentioned, have recently developed that sour flavor. </p>
<p>Since sometime around 2014ish, I've been watching the world of news and politics through the lens of someone who spent a lot of time building Web technologies and social media campaigns. As a result, I picked up on the fake news machine previous to the 2016 election. Corporate giants have largely consolidated Web  platforms into a few major players nearly erasing what we once called the "Open Web". In many cases these platforms have become an extension of traditional corporate run media (tv, radio, etc) rather than a counterpoint to them. Rather than being the technology that frees us, the Web seems to have become and extension of the same trap.</p>

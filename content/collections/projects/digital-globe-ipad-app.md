Built at Mondo Robot: an interactive iPad experience teaching Digital Globe's clients how satellite systems work — how data waits on the satellite until a ground station passes underneath, how two scans from different angles combine into a 3D scene. Networked so a live presenter controlled the pacing.

The design challenge was making complex technical concepts tangible.

Originally built for Gathering of Developers, the platform was effective enough that after the Take-Two acquisition, Take2Games.com was rebuilt on it. It powered the publisher's primary web presence for more than a decade.

Also included: a product portal hub for all GodGames titles and a private B2B community for game retailers.

The Temple of Moon was built in 2011 for Apogaea, Colorado's regional Burning Man event, after a beloved community member named Moon and her unborn child died. Moon was a founding member of the Phoenix Asylum makerspace and lead artist on CANO's Circus of Fear. The temple was designed as a bridge with spiral staircases on each end, forming an infinity symbol when viewed from above. Each end had six steps, symbolic of the tattoos on Moon's face.

I had no prior large-scale art experience. I drew on carpentry skills learned helping build my family's home during high school, built at Phoenix Asylum with access to tools and a community of talented makers, and organized volunteers through a community group before even submitting the art grant proposal.

The burn included a beautiful accident: the hollow center posts for the spiral stairs, intended for airflow, superheated fuel when I forgot to drill air holes in the base, creating 14-foot flaming torches. The Temple of Moon was later featured in one of Burning Man's official calendars.

Muenster Telephone Company launched consumer Internet service for its small rural Texas community under the brand North Texas Information Network (NTIN), and Scott was the very first technical support representative hired. At the dawn of the commercial Internet era, he was the person helping a small Texas town get online for the first time. He left this role to join Gathering of Developers.

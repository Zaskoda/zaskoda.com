I founded Partavate Studios as an interactive media production company focused on the intersection of entertainment and emerging technologies. The flagship product was Orbiter 8, a decentralized space trading MMO built entirely on the Ethereum Virtual Machine: all game logic in Solidity smart contracts, no central server. A secondary product, PolyDice, shipped as an award-winning EthDenver 2022 entry built with Rix Studios.

As CEO I recruited co-founders and presented at blockchain and gaming conferences. Orbiter 8 was deployed and tested across more than ten chains, won two hackathons, and received a grant from Polygon Studios.
